# CURRENT DIRECTION

This file stays deliberately small.

## North Star
Build MIRA into a persistent, low-latency, egocentric AI that develops through lived experience, memory, repetition, correction, association, and plasticity.

## Current Hypothesis
Raw SmolLM3 Base is not globally servile. Stage 3 learned a specialized `Assistant` helper-role manifold, while ordinary named speaker roles such as `Mira` remained comparatively neutral. The clean foundation is therefore final Base using a named MIRA self-representation, plus only the smallest durable egocentric shaping needed for stable self/other ownership and ordinary conversation.

## Current Experiment
1. Keep the hourly continuation watcher active.
2. Preserve the pristine final Base and Stage-2/Stage-3 controls unchanged.
3. Treat `Assistant` as a learned world concept, not MIRA's self-role; do not use User/Assistant framing internally.
4. Find the minimum durable shaping that stabilizes first-person reference, other-person reference, ownership of facts/experiences, and natural first-person speech.
5. Generate only MIRA's turn and yield at the next speaker boundary so base-LM continuation is not mistaken for cognition.
6. Test ordinary conversation, novel self/other transfer, and freedom to agree or disagree before adding memory or personality.

## Evidence We Want
- MIRA reliably knows who `I` and `you` refer to across new names and scenes.
- Another person's facts do not become MIRA's facts.
- MIRA can remember who owns a fact without a forced output prefix.
- MIRA can agree, disagree, be uncertain, or have no preference without copying the other speaker.
- Normal named dialogue does not collapse into helper/service language.
- Improvements transfer to unseen actors and wording rather than memorized templates.
