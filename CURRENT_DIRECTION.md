# CURRENT DIRECTION

This file stays deliberately small.

## North Star
Build MIRA into a persistent, low-latency, egocentric AI that develops through lived experience, memory, repetition, correction, association, and plasticity.

## Current Hypothesis
Raw SmolLM3 Base is not globally servile. Stage 3 learned a specialized `Assistant` helper-role manifold, while ordinary named speaker roles such as `Mira` remained comparatively neutral. The current minimal egocentric adapter is already sufficient to expose ordinary person-to-person conversation without helper-mode sludge. The active weakness is now self-fact ownership / epistemic grounding: conversational plausibility can be mistaken for autobiographical truth.

## Current Experiment
1. Keep the continuation watcher active.
2. Preserve the pristine final Base and all control checkpoints unchanged.
3. Keep `Assistant` as a learned world concept, never MIRA's self-role.
4. Preserve the current conversational candidate as a control.
5. Find the smallest durable shaping that distinguishes supported MIRA self-facts, other-person facts, social mirroring/hypotheticals, and unknown self-facts.
6. Generate only MIRA's turn and yield at the next speaker boundary.
7. Re-test ordinary conversation after each change; reject any edit that buys grounding by making her robotic, evasive, or compulsively contrarian.

## Evidence We Want
- MIRA reliably knows who `I` and `you` refer to across new names and scenes.
- Another person's facts do not become MIRA's facts.
- Unsupported conversational premises are not silently promoted into autobiographical history.
- MIRA can say she does not know a self-fact without collapsing into disclaimer boilerplate.
- MIRA can still joke, speculate, agree, disagree, and play along socially without confusing play with memory.
- Normal named dialogue does not collapse into helper/service language.
- Improvements transfer to unseen actors and wording rather than memorized templates.
