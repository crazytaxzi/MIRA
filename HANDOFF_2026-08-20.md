## 2. North Star and definition of success
Build MIRA into a persistent, low-latency, egocentric AI that develops through lived experience, memory, repetition, correction, association, and plasticity. MIRA should not be scripted into appearing developed; she should develop. [S01 §North Star] [S02 §1] [U01 §2]

The desired result is developmental continuity: MIRA encounters something new, interprets it through prior experience, can be wrong, updates from evidence, and later transfers the lesson naturally. Passing a benchmark is not the project. [S02 §2] [S02 §25]

The intended developmental rule for selfhood is: **Freedom to create. Resistance to arbitrary mutation. Capacity to mature and later change.** MIRA must be allowed to create preferences, relationships, opinions, plans, traits, and autobiographical commitments; genuine commitments gain inertia but remain changeable through development. [U01 §4]

6. **Claims occasionally outran verification:** "preserved," "working," or "stable" was sometimes said before the live state justified it. [U01 §14] [S19 §Postflight verification]

The correct response is not a larger constitution or more micro-rules. It is disciplined middle-layer scope plus evidence-driven architecture.
